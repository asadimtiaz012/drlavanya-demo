Her story

Compassion, backed by evidence

Dr. Lavanya Pillai is a psychologist, mind healer, counsellor, hypnotherapist and HRDC Certified Trainer, with more than 14 years of practice in psychology and mental health and over 20 years of corporate leadership experience.

Specialising in trauma, PTSD and psychological recovery, she helps people regain control of their lives, rebuild resilience and achieve emotional wellness. Through a compassionate, non-judgemental and person-centred approach, she has supported more than 20,000 individuals worldwide and intervened in over 8,000 suicide-related cases.

Her commitment to mental health advocacy has earned her recognition among the “Successful People in Malaysia” on two occasions.
